Helicopter Trim Solver and Sensitivity Analysis

Flight DynamicsMATLABDynamic ModelingControl
On this page Overview Contributions Methods / System Results ← Back home

Overview

This project developed a nonlinear helicopter trim solver for steady forward flight and used it to study how design parameters affect aircraft attitude, control input, and power requirement.

Key Contributions

  • Developed a nonlinear trim solver in MATLAB.
  • Implemented induced inflow calculation, rotor flapping dynamics, aerodynamic coefficient estimation, and force/moment equilibrium.
  • Performed sensitivity analyses on center-of-gravity location, horizontal tail configuration, tail rotor position, and flapping frequency.

Methods / System

  • Mathematical model: six-equation force and moment equilibrium.
  • Numerical method: iterative trim solution with aerodynamic coefficient updates.
  • Sensitivity variables: CG, tail configuration, rotor parameters, and flapping frequency.

Results and Next Steps

  • Evaluated how design parameters influence control input, aircraft attitude, rotor flapping response, and power consumption.
  • Future updates: add plots of trim variables and sensitivity curves.
