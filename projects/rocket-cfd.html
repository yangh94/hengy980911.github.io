Overview

This project simulated methane–oxygen rocket engine internal flow using ANSYS Fluent to analyze thermofluid behavior inside the combustion chamber and converging–diverging nozzle.

Key Contributions

  • Designed rocket engine geometry in ANSYS DesignModeler.
  • Modeled internal reacting flow to analyze pressure, temperature, velocity distribution, and nozzle acceleration.
  • Performed mesh independence and convergence studies to improve solution reliability.

Methods / System

  • Geometry: combustion chamber and converging–diverging nozzle.
  • Simulation: turbulence modeling, energy equation, reacting flow setup, and convergence monitoring.
  • Analysis: thermal gradients, pressure distribution, and nozzle acceleration.

Results and Next Steps

  • Produced CFD results describing internal engine flow trends and thermal behavior.
  • Future updates: add contour plots and mesh independence figures.
