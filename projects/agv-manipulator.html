AGV System with Manipulator

Overview

This thesis designed and simulated an automated guided vehicle integrated with an RPRR robotic manipulator for material handling applications.

Key Contributions

  • Designed the AGV structure and RPRR manipulator architecture.
  • Performed torque analysis, structural design, stress analysis, and strength verification.
  • Developed an A*-based global path planning framework with weighted heuristics.
  • Evaluated manipulator workspace, payload capability, and AGV navigation performance.

Methods / System

  • Mechanical design: AGV chassis and RPRR manipulator.
  • Analysis: actuator torque estimation, structural strength, and finite element verification.
  • Planning: weighted A* planner for improved computational efficiency.

Results and Next Steps

  • Improved computational efficiency by 15% while maintaining path optimality across tested scenarios.
  • Received Outstanding Bachelor Thesis recognition.
